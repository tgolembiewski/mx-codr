# WF02 — every workflow task outcome is chosen in a test that signs in as both people

## What it checks
Every outcome of every workflow task (approve, reject…) is chosen in a test, and the test logs in as both people: the one who starts the workflow and the one who decides.

## Fix
Start the flow as one demo user, `sign_in_as('<the targeted user>')`, choose the outcome, sign back in as the first and assert what they see; one test per outcome.
